<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/sum-of-beauty-of-all-substrings-1662962118/1">Sum of Beauty of All Substrings</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a string <strong>s</strong>, return the sum of the beauty of all substrings of s. </span><span style="font-size: 18px;">The beauty of a string is the difference between the frequency of its most frequent character and the frequency of its least frequent character among characters that appear in the string. </span><span style="font-size: 18px;">For example, the beauty of "aaac" is 3 - 1 = 2.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre style="background: #eeeeee; border: 1px solid #cccccc; padding: 5px 10px; --darkreader-inline-bgimage: initial; --darkreader-inline-bgcolor: #222426; --darkreader-inline-border-top: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-right: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-bottom: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-left: #3e4446;"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "aaac"<br><strong>Output: </strong>3<br><strong>Explanation:&nbsp;</strong></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">The substrings with non-zero beauty are "aaac" and "aac". The beauty of "aaac" is 2 and the beauty of "aac" is 1. Therefore, the sum is 3.</span></pre>
<pre style="background: #eeeeee; border: 1px solid #cccccc; padding: 5px 10px; --darkreader-inline-bgimage: initial; --darkreader-inline-bgcolor: #222426; --darkreader-inline-border-top: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-right: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-bottom: #3e4446; --darkreader-inline-border-left: #3e4446;"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>s = "geeksforgeeks"<br><strong>Output: </strong>62<br><strong>Explanation:</strong> </span><span style="font-size: 18px;">The sum of the beauty values of all substrings is 62.</span></pre>
